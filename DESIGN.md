7. Important professional positioning considerations
There are a few things I would deliberately manage as the website develops.

1. My current institutional position

My experience at ICWRGC and the work with the International Soil Moisture Network are important professional credentials. They should be clearly reflected in my biography and career history.

However, distinguish my personal professional activities from institutional activities. In particular, make sure any external consulting, advisory work, use of institutional materials or references to my current role comply with fair standards.

2. Research supervision

I don't hold a formal university appointment, so use language such as research mentoring, academic collaboration and methodological guidance. This still communicates my capabilities without overstating my academic position.

3. Strategic advisory
This is an important longer-term positioning opportunity. I would explicitly invite opportunities to serve on:
- Scientific and technical advisory committees.
- Water resources and environmental expert panels.
- International research and development working groups.
- Institutional technical advisory boards.
- Water security and climate resilience initiatives.
These are credible professional engagement pathways to develop over time.
