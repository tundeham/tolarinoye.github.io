Featured · Scientific Data, 2020

WoKaS: the first global open database of karst spring discharge

Karst aquifers supply drinking water to a large part of the world's population, yet their behaviour was hard to compare because observations were scattered across literature, agencies and research groups. WoKaS brings these records together, harmonised and openly available, so that researchers and water managers can learn from karst systems in contrasting climates.
